SCIENCE & TELEMETRY

Atmospheric optics

What we measure

Temperature and pressure provide the descent profile. Spectral light and UV readings investigate changes with altitude, using colour ratios, orientation correction and a ground reference.

LoRa carries the 1 Hz backup telemetry; the planned 4G link sends the full-rate log. These are design budgets, not a live flight feed.

Simple Rayleigh + haze model for planning. Actual accuracy depends on calibration, pointing, weather and canopy shading.
